The Statutory Framework: Class 1 Exemptions Explained

In Dublin City, Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown, South Dublin, and Fingal, the 40-square-metre rear extension exemption is the most powerful tool available to homeowners looking to modernise 1930s to 1980s properties without waiting 4 to 6 months for local council approval.

Governed by Schedule 2, Part 1, Class 1 of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001, this exemption allows single-storey extensions to the rear of a dwelling house, provided four fundamental statutory conditions are strictly maintained.

The Four Non-Negotiable Conditions

1. 25m² Minimum Private Garden SpaceAt least 25 square metres of private open amenity space must remain in the rear garden after the extension is complete. Driveways and side passages do not qualify.
2. Height Restrictions & Eaves AlignmentThe height of the extension walls cannot exceed the existing eaves height of the main dwelling. If constructing a flat roof with parapet, the top of the parapet must not breach this statutory datum.
3. Cumulative Historic CalculationsAny previous extension built by prior owners since the house was constructed must be deducted from your 40m² allowance.
4. No Side or Front VisibilityExempt extensions must be located strictly to the rear of the property. Side extensions require standard planning permission.

Statutory Questions Answered by Master Builder Sérgio Gratival

Q: Does a previous extension reduce my 40m² allowance?

Yes. The 40m² limit is cumulative for the life of the property. If a previous owner added a 15m² sunroom, you have 25m² of remaining exempt development space.

Q: Can I build a two-storey extension without planning permission in Dublin?

Yes, but with strict caveats: the total area cannot exceed 40m² (combined ground and first floor), and any first-floor extension must maintain a minimum 2-metre setback from boundaries to prevent overlooking.

Q: Do I still need Building Control (BCAR) for an exempt extension?

Yes. Planning exemption only exempts you from the planning application; it does NOT exempt you from Irish Building Regulations (Technical Guidance Documents Part A to M).
