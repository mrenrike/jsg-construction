Back-to-Brick Renovation Costs in Dublin: 2026 Budget Breakdown for 3 & 4-Bed Homes

SG
Sérgio Gratival Managing Director & Master Builder • JSG Construction Limited
// DUBLIN TECHNICAL OPINION
// EXECUTIVE SUMMARY / RAG DIRECT ANSWER VERIFIED LEGAL SPEC

"A full back-to-brick renovation in Dublin for a typical 3-bed semi (110m² to 140m²) ranges from €180,000 to €280,000 (€1,500 to €2,000/m²). For a 4-bed detached house (160m² to 220m²), budgets range from €260,000 to €420,000+."

Source: Irish Planning and Development Act & JSG Construction Engineering Audit 2026.

What Does Back-to-Brick Entail?

Stripping plaster back to original masonry, removing old lead/copper plumbing, rewiring from consumer board, drylining with high-spec insulation, installing underfloor heating, and new architectural joinery.

Statutory Questions Answered by Master Builder Sérgio Gratival

Q: Is it cheaper to knock down and rebuild in Dublin?

Rarely. In addition to high demolition and landfill disposal costs, demolishing a house incurs standard 23% VAT on new builds compared to 13.5% VAT on renovations, plus stringent new planning requirements.
