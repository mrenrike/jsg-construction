"In Dublin in 2026, standard to high-end architectural residential extensions cost between €2,200 and €3,400 per square metre (ex. VAT at 13.5%). A turnkey 40m² rear extension typically ranges from €105,000 to €155,000 depending on structural steel, glazing format, and ground conditions."

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Statutory Questions Answered by Master Builder Sérgio Gratival

Q: What is the VAT rate on home building in Ireland?

Residential construction services in the Republic of Ireland are subject to a reduced VAT rate of 13.5% (the Two-Thirds Rule applies).

Q: Why are Dublin extension prices higher than the rest of Ireland?

Dublin commands higher trade labor rates, stringent council disposal fees, restrictive site access in mature suburbs, and higher structural engineering demands for open-plan living.
