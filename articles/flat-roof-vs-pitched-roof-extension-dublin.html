The Modern Warm Roof Assembly

Warm roof construction places continuous rigid PIR insulation on top of the structural timber deck, keeping the joists warm and eliminating any possibility of interstitial condensation in Dublin's damp winter climate.

Q: Do flat roofs still leak in Ireland?

Modern warm flat roofs using continuous EPDM rubber or polyurea single-ply membranes with zero seams have a lifespan of 30+ years and eliminate the pooling water issues of 1970s felt roofs.
