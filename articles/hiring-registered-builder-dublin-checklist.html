Q: What is CIRI in Ireland?

The Construction Industry Register Ireland (CIRI) is the official government register of competent, tax-compliant, and insured construction companies.

Q: Why should I never pay cash upfront?

Rogue builders request upfront deposits and disappear. Legitimate master builders like JSG work strictly on verified milestone stage payments under RIAI contracts.
