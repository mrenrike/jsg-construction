What are the exact rules for the 40m² planning exemption in Dublin?

Under Irish law (Planning and Development Regulations 2001, Class 1 Exemptions), you can extend up to 40 square metres (430 sq. ft) to the rear of your home without formal planning permission. However, strict statutory preconditions must be met to preserve your exemption:

  • > You must maintain at least 25m² of private rear garden open space.
  • > Extension wall height cannot exceed existing roof eaves; pitch must conform.
  • > Prior extensions count cumulatively toward the 40m² ceiling.
