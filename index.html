The questions that come up first

Straight answers, and the guide to read when the answer depends on your site.

Do I need planning permission for an extension in Dublin?

Not always. A rear extension can fall under the exempted development rules when it stays inside the limits on floor area, height and remaining private open space, and when the house has not used that allowance already. The conditions are specific and worth reading before you design anything.

Read the 40 m² exemption rules

How much does a home extension cost in Dublin?

It depends on the structure underneath, not on the square metres alone: ground conditions, whether steel is needed, the glazing specification and the level of finish. We price it itemised after a site visit, so you see what each element costs before anything is agreed.

Read the cost breakdown

Who signs the build off under Irish building regulations?

Work of this size falls under BCAR, the Building Control Amendment Regulations, which require the design and the construction to be certified. JSG Construction works to BCAR and under RIAI contracts on every project.

Read what BCAR means for a homeowner

Do you use subcontractors?

The core crew is eight in-house craftsmen who move from job to job together, rather than a chain of subcontractors assembled for each build. Specialist trades that genuinely need a certificate, such as gas and electrical, are brought in and signed off.

Are you insured?

Yes. JSG Construction Limited carries €6.5M public liability cover, which applies to every project and every stage of the build.

Which areas do you build in?

Dublin city and county, and the neighbouring parts of Kildare and Wicklow. Site visits run Monday to Friday.
